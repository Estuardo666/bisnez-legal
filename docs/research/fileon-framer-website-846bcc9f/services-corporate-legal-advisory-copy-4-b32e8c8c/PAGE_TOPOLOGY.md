# Service detail topology

Source: `https://fileon.framer.website/services/corporate-legal-advisory-copy-4`

## Desktop, 1440 px

1. Light navigation in normal document flow, approximately 100 px tall.
2. Intro grid, 1350 px wide: white editorial panel on the left and a 645 × 700 image on the right.
3. Left panel: eyebrow, 64/73.6 title, short summary, pill CTA and two equal metric cards along the bottom.
4. Long-form article, approximately 1080 px wide, with three text groups before a 2.04:1 image and two text groups after it.
5. Centered back button.
6. Dark site footer.

## Tablet and mobile

- Navigation collapses to logo plus menu button.
- Intro becomes a vertical stack: copy, individual metric cards, then the image.
- Title is 40/46 px.
- Article and media become full width with 40 px outer gutters on mobile.
- No sticky or scroll-driven content; layout is normal flow.
